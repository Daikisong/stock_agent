Copper Price Expected to Reach All-time High Seen in 2011
The author is an analyst of NH Investment & Securities. He can be reached at will.byun@nhqv.com. -- Ed.
Having risen strongly, the price of copper is nearing US$9,000/ton. Despite its conservative guidance, Poongsan’s share price should climb in response to copper price expansion. We expect the price of copper to reclaim its all-time high of US$10,500/ton.
Raise TP to reflect copper price increase
Adhering to a Buy rating, we raise our TP on Poongsan by 19.4% to W43,000, a level equivalent to a 2021E P/B of 0.8x (ROE of 6.2%). We expect the firm to log 2021 OP of W158.1bn (+30.4% y-y). With the price of copper nearing US$9,000/ton, profit improvement is forecast at the copper product business alongside anticipated share price growth. On the other hand, while exports should pick up at the defense business, overall defense sales will likely rise only 1.4% y-y due to domestic sales decline.
We assume an average 2021 copper price of US$8,758/ton (+41.8% y-y), a figure 10.9% higher versus our previous assumption. We also hike 2021E EPS by 14.3%. Poongsan should record consolidated 2021 sales of W3,048bn (+17.5% y-y), OP of W158.1bn (+30.4% y-y), and NP (excluding minority interests) of W91.7bn (+27.6% y-y). On a quarterly basis, OP should remain favorable at W34.4bn (TTP y-y, -35.7% q-q) in 1Q21 and W45.4bn (+116.3% y-y, +32.2% q-q) in 2Q21.
Despite conservative guidance, strong copper price momentum to push up share price
Poongsan’s 2021 guidance targets non-consolidated sales of W2.2tn and OP of W109.4bn. While these figures represent increases of 14.4% and 39.6%, respectively, compared to last year’s numbers, they remain below our estimates and the market projections. Assuming an average 2021 copper price of US$7,500/ton, Poongsan’s guidance looks conservative, in our view. However, despite the soft guidance, Poongsan’s share price should climb moving ahead in response to copper price expansion.
As of Feb 19, the price of copper had strengthened to US$8,947/ton, backed by accelerating demand stemming from economic recovery, low inventory levels, and slow supply increase from mines. We expect the price of copper to reach US$10,500/ton, the all-time high seen in Feb 2011, when Poongsan’s share price rose to W52,600. If high demand driven by economic recovery and record-low inventory levels continues to push up copper prices, Poongsan’s share price increase will likely outpace its earnings growth.
