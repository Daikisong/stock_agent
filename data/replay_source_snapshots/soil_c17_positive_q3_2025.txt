S-OIL Corporation Q3 2025 Results.
Backed by favorable fuel and lube spreads, operating income reached 229.2 billion Won, making a substantial earnings turnaround.
S-OIL Refining segment turned to profit thanks to fading one-off factors in Q2 and robust refining margins.
S-OIL Operating Income 229.2 bil. Won in Q3 25; Q2 25 -344.0 bil. Won; Turn to Profit.
S-OIL Refining operating income 115.5 bil. Won in Q3 25; Q2 25 -441.1 bil. Won; Turn to Profit.
S-OIL Regional refining margins strengthened, led by middle distillate, amid tight supply caused by Russian refinery disruptions.
S-OIL CDU utilization rate was 97% in Q3 2025 and RFCC/HYC utilization rate was 98%.
